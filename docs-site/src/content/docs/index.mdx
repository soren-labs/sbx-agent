## How it works

**1. Deploy once into your Modal workspace**

Everything runs under your account: control plane, sandboxes, images, durable storage. One `sbx deploy` command creates the App, mounts your credentials as Secrets, and boots the control plane.

**2. Create a task with one API call**

`POST /v1/tasks` with a prompt, an optional repository and a delivery target. The control plane resolves the repo to an exact commit, picks a provider and account with a free slot, and starts the run.

**3. Watch, follow up, deliver**

Stream run events over SSE or poll the task ledger. Follow-up runs resume the provider's native session. When the task touched a repository, publish the revision as a branch or pull request, record a review, and merge.

## What it's not

⚠️ **Read before deploying.** sbx-browser is orchestration for your own agents, not a hosted service.

- **BYO Modal:** Every control plane and sandbox runs in *your* Modal workspace and costs you.
- **BYO subscriptions:** Agents authenticate with *your* official provider accounts (Codex, Devin, etc.). sbx-browser does not resell, proxy, or convert quota—tasks execute inside the provider's own CLI.
- **No quotas or accounts:** We do not store model weights, host user accounts, or call provider APIs directly. Your credentials live in your Modal workspace and are injected into your sandboxes only.
