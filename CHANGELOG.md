# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — Unified Session architecture

Breaking replacement of legacy product/API/state machinery with one PostgreSQL-backed business API, typed Console and Python SDK/CLI. Durable Sessions, Messages, Turns, replaceable ExecutorLeases, official OpenCode Harness, immutable ChangeSets, durable Delivery and ordinary Delegation child Sessions now share events and fenced Jobs.

Manual encrypted Modal/GitHub/Zen connections and email/password identity replace ambient account bridges. The legacy Task/Run, hosted/V1/V2, review/revision/workflow engines and old Console are removed. Old SDK imports/routes are intentionally unavailable. Consult the RFC and implementation report for supported capabilities, acceptance evidence and migration limits.
